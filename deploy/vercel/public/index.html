About this system

An AI quality-inspection system for packaged ICs. A YOLO11n model finds every device in the camera frame; each device is cropped and a YOLO11n-seg model outlines five defect types (crack, scratch, contamination, chip, missing pin). Each defect is measured in millimetres (12 px/mm camera calibration) and quality rules decide PASS, REWORK or SCRAP.

The models were trained by the author on a synthetic dataset of 750 generated camera frames (1,459 devices) and are served here as ONNX files by a FastAPI function. On the held-out test set (307 devices) this deployment makes the correct decision for 87.9 % of devices.

Academic prototype trained on synthetic images — not a manufacturing quality-control decision. Source: github.com/BALASUDHAN1807/WaferSense-AI · Author: Balasudhan R
