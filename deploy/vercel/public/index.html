  1. Find devicesYOLO11n locates every IC package in the camera frame.
  2. Outline defectsYOLO11n-seg masks cracks, scratches, contamination, chips and missing pins.
  3. MeasureMasks become area and length in millimetres at 12 px/mm.
  4. DecideQuality rules give PASS, REWORK or SCRAP per device.

Results

The IC models were trained on 750 generated camera frames (1,459 devices) and are served here as ONNX files by a FastAPI function. On the held-out test set of 307 devices this deployment makes the correct decision for 96.1 % of them; a second training stage that oversampled scratches and missing pins raised this from 87.9 %.

On real MVTec AD transistor photos, PatchCore anomaly detection plus a defect-type classifier gets 86.7 % of held-out verdicts right.

Academic prototype trained mostly on synthetic images; not a manufacturing quality-control decision. Source: github.com/BALASUDHAN1807/WaferSense-AI. Author: Balasudhan R.
